## Languages

The product is available in six languages:

| Language | Shown as |
| --- | --- |
| English | English |
| Spanish | Español |
| German | Deutsch |
| French | Français |
| Portuguese | Português |
| Chinese | 中文 |

### How your language is chosen

We use the first of these that applies:

1. The language saved on your account, once you are signed in.
2. The language you chose earlier on this device.
3. Your browser's language.
4. Your device's language (mobile app).
5. English.

Dates, numbers and money are shown the way your language writes them, for example `£19.99`.

<Callout type="warn" title="Translations are still being checked">
  Languages other than English have not yet been reviewed by native speakers. If something reads
  oddly, please [tell us](/docs/help).
</Callout>

## Light and dark

You can switch between light and dark. On this documentation site, use the toggle in the header.
