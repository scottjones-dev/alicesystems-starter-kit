We measure how the product is used so we can improve it, and we are careful about what that includes.

## What we do

- Count what happens in the product, such as "a sign-in completed", to see what works.
- Keep that usage data in the **European Union**.
- Remove anything that could identify you from usage data before it is sent: email addresses, names, phone numbers, notes, passwords and tokens.
- Remove the private parts of one-time links, such as sign-in links, from the page addresses we record.

## What we never do

- Send your email address, name, phone number or notes to our analytics.
- Record your screen or take screenshots of the app.

## Errors

If the product crashes, a report is sent so we can fix it. Personal data is removed from these reports too.

## The full policy

Our privacy policy and terms are on the [website](/docs/website). They are the legal documents; this page is a plain summary.
