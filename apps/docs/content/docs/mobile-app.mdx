The mobile app is for work away from a desk, on phones and tablets. It is designed to keep working when your connection is poor.

<Callout title="Coming soon">
  The app is not released yet. Install steps and features will be listed here at launch.
</Callout>

## What to expect

- The same account as the [web app](/docs/web-app).
- The same language choices, see [Language and appearance](/docs/language-and-appearance).
- Push notifications for events that need your attention, if you allow them in your device's settings.

## Notifications on your device

To stop push notifications, turn them off for the app in your device's settings. Account emails, such as sign-in links, are still sent by email.
