## Signing in

You will be able to sign in with an **email link**: enter your email address and we send you a link that signs you in.

- The link works once and expires after a short time.
- Open it on the device you want to sign in on.
- If it does not arrive, see [Help and support](/docs/help).

## What is always available

- [Language and appearance](/docs/language-and-appearance): choose your language, and light or dark.
- [Emails and notifications](/docs/notifications): the messages you will receive.
