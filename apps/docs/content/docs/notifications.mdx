We message you about your account and your work. Each message is written in [your language](/docs/language-and-appearance).

## Emails about your account

These are account and security messages, because you need them to use your account.

| Email | When you get it | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-in link | You ask to sign in | Open the link on the device you are signing in on. It works once. |
| Verify your email | You add or change an email address | Open the link to confirm the address is yours. |
| Reset your password | You ask to reset a password | Open the link and choose a new one. |
| Organisation invitation | Someone invites you to join their organisation | Open the link to accept, or ignore it to decline. |
| Delete your account | You ask to delete your account | Open the link to confirm. |

<Callout type="warn" title="Did not ask for it?">
  If you receive a sign-in, reset or deletion email you did not ask for, ignore it. Nothing
  changes unless the link is opened. Contact [support](/docs/help) if it keeps happening.
</Callout>

## Push notifications

The [mobile app](/docs/mobile-app) can send short push notifications for events that need your attention. They do not contain sensitive detail. Turn them off in your device's settings.
