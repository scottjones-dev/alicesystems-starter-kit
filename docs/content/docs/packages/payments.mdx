**Status:** planned, build order 15. **Design from:** new. next-forge's `payments` is a Stripe client wrapper for charging its own customers, which is the other half of the problem (below).

## Two different jobs called "payments"

1. **Platform billing: our tenants pay us** for the software. This is handled by the Better Auth Stripe plugin inside `@repo/auth`: one subscription per organization.
2. **Tenant collections: our tenants collect money from their customers.** A pub taking card payments, a nursery collecting fees by direct debit, a field service company invoicing a customer. **This package.**

They have different keys, different webhooks and different legal duties, so they stay apart. The money manager does not collect payments; it reads bank accounts (see `banking`).

## Requirements

- Tenants receive the money themselves: our platform must not hold their funds.
- We never see or store card numbers.
- Every provider call is safe to retry (idempotency keys), and every webhook is safe to receive twice.
- A payment moves through known states and an impossible move is refused.
- The pub's card terminal keeps working when the internet drops, within the terminal's own offline rules.

## Design

- **A `PaymentProvider` interface** with two adapters: **Stripe** (online cards, Terminal readers for the pub, Connect so each tenant has their own Stripe account) and **GoCardless** (Direct Debit mandates and collections, for nursery fees and recurring invoices).
- **State machine:** `pending`, `authorised`, `paid`, `failed`, `refunded` and `disputed`, each move written to the audit log and every amount a `Money` value.
- **Inbound webhooks live here:** verify the signature, record the provider's event id first (so a duplicate is ignored), then hand processing to `@repo/jobs` and answer quickly.
- **Reconciliation job** compares our records with the provider's daily and reports differences.
- **Keys:** one Stripe platform account serves both billing and Connect; the GoCardless token is per environment. Both are optional and the package stays off without them.

## Order cost

Payments records the organization as a plain id until auth adds the foreign key, as `audit` does.

## Trade-offs

- Two providers mean two sets of states and webhooks mapped onto one. The alternative, Stripe only, would miss direct debit, which is what UK nurseries and recurring invoices want.
- Connect gives tenants their own accounts and keeps us out of holding money, at the cost of tenant onboarding steps and provider fees.

## Open questions

- Connect account type (Standard, Express or Custom) and whether we take a platform fee.
- Strong Customer Authentication behaviour for UK cards, tested in Stripe's test mode.
- Whether a licensed partner is needed for anything beyond what Stripe and GoCardless already cover. Check this before launch; I am not giving legal advice.
