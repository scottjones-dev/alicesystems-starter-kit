## What it is

`apps/website` is the public face of the product: what it is, pricing and the legal pages. It was created with `pnpm create next-app` (App Router, `src/` directory, Tailwind) and fitted into the monorepo.

## Requirements

- Fast and indexable by search engines, with no sign-in.
- The product name, description and languages come from `@repo/config`, never typed into pages.
- Deployable and cacheable on its own, separate from the signed-in app.

## Design

- **Separate from the platform.** The marketing site and the product have different jobs, caches, release timing and security needs, so they are two apps. The cost is two deployments.
- **Next.js App Router with static pages** wherever possible.
- **Workspace packages are compiled with the app** through `transpilePackages`, because they ship TypeScript source.
- **Port 3001** in development (the platform is 3000).
- The scaffold's own `pnpm-workspace.yaml`, `biome.json` and pinned TypeScript were removed: the repo root owns the workspace, lint and the TypeScript version, and `tsconfig.json` extends `@repo/typescript-config/nextjs.json`.

## Trade-offs

- Two Next.js apps repeat some setup. Shared pieces move into packages (`@repo/ui`, `@repo/config`) rather than being copied.
